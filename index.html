About me

A student who codes and designs.

I'm Arbrent John Dumat-ol, a 23-year-old 3rd year student taking the Bachelor of Science in Information Technology (BSINT) at Negros Oriental State University – Bayawan-Sta. Catalina Campus (NORSU-BSC).

Alongside my IT studies, I do graphic design: illustrations, posters, logos and publication layouts using Adobe Illustrator, InDesign and other creative tools.

I enjoy the space where design meets technology, making things that look good and work well.

Word Blaster Typing Game

An interactive typing game I built in Adobe Animate and exported to HTML5 Canvas. Words fall from space, and you blast them by typing each one correctly before it reaches the bottom. It mixes animation with game logic: scoring, levels and keyboard input.
