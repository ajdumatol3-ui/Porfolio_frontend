About me

A student who codes and designs.

I'm Arbrent John Dumat-ol, a 3rd year student taking the Bachelor of Science in Information Technology (BSINT) at Negros Oriental State University – Bayawan-Sta. Catalina Campus (NORSU-BSC).

I'm also a web developer. I build responsive websites with HTML, CSS and JavaScript, like this portfolio, focusing on clean layouts, smooth animations and pages that work well on both desktop and mobile.

Alongside my IT studies, I do graphic design: illustrations, posters, logos and publication layouts using Adobe Illustrator, InDesign and other creative tools.

I'm also a traditional artist. I draw anime-style characters by hand with graphite, colored pencils and ballpoint pen, and some of my drawings have been shown in a school exhibit.

I enjoy the space where design meets technology, making things that look good and work well.
